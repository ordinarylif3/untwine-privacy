Untwine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

Overview

Untwine is a puzzle game that respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

Untwine does not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or internet connection to play.

Local Storage

Your game progress (current level and best scores) is saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Third-Party Services

Untwine uses Google Play or the Apple App Store for in-app purchases. These platforms may collect data according to their own privacy policies. We do not receive any personal information from these transactions.

Children's Privacy

Untwine does not collect data from any user, including children. The app is safe for all ages.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because no data is sent to us, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Deleting the app from your device removes all locally saved progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page with the new date above.

Contact

Questions? Reach out at support@untwine.app.

Untwine is available on Google Play. Need help? See the support page.
