Untwine — Support

A cozy puzzle game. Untangle colorful strands across concentric rings.

How do I play?

Tap a ball, then tap an empty space to move it. Rearrange the balls until no strands cross. Undo is free and unlimited; if you're stuck, Hint shows the next move (at the cost of a star).

I bought the full game but the levels are locked

Open the paywall (try to start any level past 45) and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the same Google or Apple account you bought with. Purchases are tied to that store account, so they carry over to a new device once restored.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Untwine has no accounts and no cloud sync, so progress does not transfer between devices, and uninstalling the app erases it. Reinstalling then restoring your purchase gets the full game back, but not the stars.

The daily puzzle didn't change

The daily puzzle rolls over at local midnight and is chosen from your device's date. If the date or time zone on your device is wrong, the puzzle may look a day off.

Refunds

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, so refund requests go through them: Google Play refunds · Apple refunds.
